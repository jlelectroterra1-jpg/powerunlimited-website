What we run on

Our two core systems.

Here is what each system actually does, in full.

An all-in-one medical billing and practice management system, tried and tested for over two decades and built to suit solo, group, and bureau practices alike. MEDEDI coordinates across medical schemes, payment solutions, and pharmacies so billing, claims, and patient administration all run from one place.

Billing & claims

  • • Real-time claim submission and ICD-10 coding
  • • Built-in medical scheme pricing to reduce rejections
  • • Claim responses handled automatically
  • • Electronic Remittance Advice (ERA) reconciliation

Patients & payments

  • • Member and benefit checks before treatment starts
  • • Instant, accurate patient quotations
  • • Multiple payment methods, reconciled automatically
  • • Online booking and digital patient registration

Reporting & growth

  • • Daily activity and financial summaries
  • • Debt recovery and outstanding-account tracking
  • • Customisable, data-driven business reports
  • • POPIA-ready with local support behind it

Practice Perfect replaces paper patient files with a full Electronic Health Record — built in South Africa, for South African practices. Open a patient's file with a click, review notes and prescriptions at a glance, and generate letters without wading through paperwork, whether you run a solo practice or a larger clinic.

Patient records

  • • Fully paperless, encrypted patient files
  • • Patient self-registration and consent capture
  • • Medical history: allergies, conditions, surgical & family history
  • • Accessible on desktop, web, or mobile

Clinical workflow

  • • Appointment diary with SMS reminders
  • • Consultation notes by template, dictation, or AI scribe
  • • Searchable ICD-10 diagnosis browser with favourites
  • • Draw and annotate on customisable diagrams

Integrations

  • • Major pathology labs: Ampath, Lancet, Pathcare & more
  • • Billing systems including MEDEDI
  • • Task management for you and your staff
  • • eScripts sent straight to leading pharmacies
